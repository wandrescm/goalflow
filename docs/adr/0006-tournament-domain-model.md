# ADR-06: Tournament domain model (structure as data, identity per league)

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-11-06, after two owner reviews)
- **Date:** 2026-11-06 (S3D1)
- **Related:** ADR-03 (modular monolith), ADR-14 (multi-tenancy; amended with this ADR, §2.11), ADR-02 (post-save pipeline), Spec 005

## 1. Context

Each league runs its tournaments differently: one league plays a single round robin, another may later add groups or a knockout stage, and a league plays a new tournament every season with many of the same teams and players. Players change teams between seasons, and past top-scorer tables must not change when they do. Results are entered after each match (Spec 006, H11) and statistics are derived from saved matches (ADR-08, pending).

The pilot covers two leagues and one tournament per league. Their exact formats are not confirmed yet; the owner's product rule is that every tournament starts with a round robin and later stages are parametrised.

Until there is a frontend (H18), the Admin loads teams, players and the fixture in bulk. The fixture is not generated by the system in this milestone.

## 2. Decision

### 2.1 Identity per league, participation per tournament

- `Equipo` and `Jugador` belong to the **league** (tenant). Their identity survives across tournaments.
- `EquipoTorneo` is a team's participation in one tournament. `InscripcionJugador` is a player's registration with one participating team in one tournament.
- Everything that happens in a tournament references the participation, not the league identity: a match references two `EquipoTorneo`; a goal (Spec 006) will reference an `InscripcionJugador`. A later transfer therefore never rewrites history: "Juan, team X, 6 goals" in 2026-I stays true when Juan plays for Y in 2026-II.
- **No transfers within a tournament:** a player has at most one registration per tournament. Leagues that allow mid-tournament transfers need a later decision (validity periods).

### 2.2 Structure as data

- A `Torneo` has **ordered phases** (`Fase`, with a unique order inside the tournament). A phase has a **type** and its parameters are data, not code. A new format is a new phase type, never a fork of the engine.
- **Types in this milestone:** only `TodosContraTodos` (round robin), with one or two legs (`vueltas`). Groups and knockout rounds, the number of teams that qualify, and carrying points over from the previous phase (PRD F-05) are added later as new types and columns. Matches already belong to a phase, so adding types does not migrate them.
- **Knockout rules (decided now, implemented later).** In a two-legged knockout tie, the default criterion is the aggregate score; if the aggregate is level, the tie is decided by a penalty shoot-out. Leagues may use other rules, so the rules are data of the tournament or phase, stored with it. Changing a league's default never rewrites the rules of a phase already configured: past phases keep the rules they were played with. Implementing knockout phases is out of scope for H09–H10 (C-05).
- A match belongs to one phase and has a **matchday number** (`jornada`, ≥ 1). The prediction game "Fecha a Fecha" groups matches by matchday.
- A bye (odd number of teams) is the absence of a match for that team in that matchday; it is not stored.

### 2.3 Aggregates

- **League catalog:** `Equipo` and `Jugador`, each its own aggregate.
- **Tournament:** `Torneo` with its `Fase`, `EquipoTorneo` and `InscripcionJugador`.
- **Match:** `Partido` is its own aggregate, because it will carry the score sheet, its version and its events (ADR-02). It references the tournament, phase and participations by identifier.

### 2.4 Keys

- Primary keys are generated by the server (ADR-14 §2.11).
- **Natural keys** used to recognise existing rows on re-import:
  - `Torneo`, `Equipo`, `Jugador`: a **code** assigned by the league, stable across imports and seasons, unique per league. A code is an opaque label kept in the league's own spreadsheet; it is never a personal identifier (no ID-card number, phone or e-mail).
  - `EquipoTorneo`: (tournament, team). `InscripcionJugador`: (tournament, player).
  - `Fase`: (tournament, order).
  - `Partido`: (phase, home participation, away participation). In a phase, an ordered pair of teams has at most one match. A postponement changes the scheduled time of the same match; it never creates a new one.
- Names are mutable attributes, never identity: two players called the same in one team are two codes.

### 2.5 Consistency enforced by the database

Relationships inside a tournament are enforced by composite foreign keys that carry the tenant **and** the tournament (ADR-14 §2.11): a match cannot reference a phase or a participation of another tournament, and a registration cannot reference a team of another tournament. Uniqueness of the natural keys is enforced by unique constraints that include the tenant. Rules that span several rows of the same import (each ordered pair once per phase, each team at most once per matchday) are validated by a pure domain service before anything is written.

### 2.6 Loading in this milestone

The Admin loads a whole tournament with one idempotent import (Spec 005): the payload is the **complete** desired state of that tournament. The import adds and updates; it never deletes. **Omitting is not deleting:** a stored item that is missing from the payload is a conflict, because it may be an accident. A team that leaves the tournament is marked as withdrawn (`Retirado`) and its scheduled matches as cancelled (`Cancelado`); rows and history are kept, and both marks can be reverted while no result exists. A cancelled match keeps its identity (its pair cannot be scheduled again in the same phase) but frees its teams' slot in that matchday, and it never produces goals, points or statistics. Withdrawal after results is decided with the score sheet (Spec 006).

## 3. Rejected alternatives

- **Team and player owned by the tournament:** simpler, but every season duplicates identities and cross-season profiles need fuzzy matching by name.
- **A fixed structure (one table per league):** fits the pilot today, but a second format would mean new code paths and migrating matches.
- **Group, knockout and qualification now:** the pilot formats are not confirmed; modelling them now would be speculative and would not be exercised by any test with real data.
- **A league-assigned code for each match:** the ordered pair inside a phase is already a stable natural key, and postponements do not change it.
- **Matching rows by name on re-import:** names repeat and are corrected; a typo fix would create a new identity.

## 4. Consequences

- **(+)** History is stable across seasons; a match and a goal always point to the participation that was valid at the time.
- **(+)** New formats are additive (new phase types) and cross-tournament mistakes are rejected by the database.
- **(−)** More tables and composite keys than a flat model; every relationship inside a tournament needs the extra `torneo_id` column.
- **(−)** Mid-tournament transfers are not supported.
- **(−)** If a league officially replays an annulled match and must keep both records, the match key needs a new decision.

## 5. Clarifications

- **C-01** *Who owns a player's identity?* The league; registrations are per tournament. Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06 (option B).
- **C-02** *Groups in the milestone?* No, until the real formats are confirmed (owner, before H16). Matches already belong to a phase. Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06.
- **C-03** *How are rows recognised on re-import?* League-assigned codes for tournament, team and player; the ordered pair per phase for matches. Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06.
- **C-04** *Transfers within a tournament?* Not supported; one registration per player per tournament, enforced by a unique constraint. Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06.
- **C-05** *How is a two-legged knockout tie decided?* By default, aggregate score, then a penalty shoot-out if level. The rules are configurable per tournament or phase and kept as historical configuration. Implementation is out of scope for H09–H10. Open for the knockout decision: whether the shoot-out result belongs to the second-leg match or to a tie entity that groups both legs. Decided by the owner on 2026-11-06.
